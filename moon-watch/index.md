## How the numbers are made

Expected flashes per hour = scope factor x [sum over active showers of k_shower x activity profile x observable fraction + sporadic constant x dark visible area]. The observable fraction is the share of a shower's lunar impacts that land on the Earth-facing night side (cos chi weighting with the horizon cut, Madiedo et al. 2015 eq. 4), from the Moon's real position, phase and libration. k for the Geminids and Taurids and the sporadic constant are fitted to NASA (2006-2008) and 2018 Geminid counts; every other shower is provisional (scaled from the Geminids, x3 band). Scope factor 10^(0.3 x (limiting mag - 10)), frozen before fitting. Rates assume your field covers the dark side as the calibration programs' did. Bands are 90 % model bands (log-normal); a count over N hours is further Poisson-scattered.

Per-city hours for any window are in `moon-watch.json`; any site: `python -m moonwatch.forecast --site lat,lon,Zone/Name`.
